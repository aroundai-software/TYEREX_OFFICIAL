# 📱 TyreX Mobile App — Quick User Manual

Welcome to **TyreX**! This simple guide shows you step-by-step how to use the mobile app daily on the workshop floor.

---

## 1. 🔑 Login & Branch Selection

1. Open the **TyreX** app on your phone.
2. Enter your **Username** and **Password**, then tap **Login**.
3. If you have access to multiple branches, tap the **Branch Name** at the top bar to select your current workshop location (e.g., *Kochi*, *Thrissur*).

---

## 2. 📝 Creating a New Job Card

When a customer drives into the workshop:

### Step 1: Vehicle & Customer Details
1. Tap the **+** (or **New Job Card**) button.
2. Enter the **Vehicle Number** (e.g., `KL07AB1234`).
   * If the vehicle visited before, customer details will autofill automatically!
   * If it's a new customer, enter the **Customer Name** and **Phone Number**.
3. Enter the current **Odometer Reading** (KM).

### Step 2: Select Services & Tyres
* **Services:** Select services needed (e.g., *Wheel Alignment*, *Wheel Balancing*, *Tyre Fitting*, *Tyre Rotation*, *Nitrogen Topup*).
* **Tyres / Products:** If the customer is buying new tyres or accessories:
  1. Tap **Select Tyre from Catalog**.
  2. Pick the **Brand**, **Size**, and **Model**.
  3. Enter the **Quantity** and price.

### Step 3: Vehicle Condition & Wheel Photos 📸
1. Tap on the car inspection diagram to mark any existing scratches or dents (optional).
2. Under **Wheel Photos (5 required)**:
   * Tap **Start Capture**.
   * Take 5 quick photos of the wheels:
     * **FL** (Front Left)
     * **FR** (Front Right)
     * **RL** (Rear Left)
     * **RR** (Rear Right)
     * **SP** (Spare Wheel)
3. *(Optional)* Add a voice note or overall vehicle photo if needed.

### Step 4: Save & Start Work
* Tap **Create Job Card**.
* The job card is assigned a unique number (e.g., `TYX2026023`) and moves to **Work in Progress**.

---

## 3. 🔧 Managing & Completing Jobs

Go to the **Job Cards** tab on the bottom menu:

* **Drafts:** Jobs saved as drafts to finish later.
* **Work in Progress:** Jobs currently being worked on by technicians.
* **Completed Jobs:** Finished jobs ready for billing.

### Completing a Job:
1. Tap on the active job card in **Work in Progress**.
2. If any extra services or parts were added during the work, add them here.
3. Tap **Complete Job**.
4. *(Optional)* Take quick "After Photos" to show the finished work.
5. Tap **Confirm Completion** — the job is now ready for billing!

---

## 4. 💳 Billing & Payment

Once the job is completed:
1. Open the job from **Completed Jobs**.
2. Tap **Generate Bill / Invoice**.
3. Verify the items, quantities, and GST rates.
4. Select the **Payment Method**:
   * **Cash**
   * **UPI / GPay / PhonePe**
   * **Card**
5. Tap **Record Payment & Generate Invoice**.
6. You can now tap **Share on WhatsApp** to send the PDF invoice directly to the customer's phone!

> 🔄 **Tally Sync:** Once billed, the job card and payment automatically sync to Tally Prime without any manual data entry!

---

## 5. 📦 Direct Sales / Courier (Without Vehicle)

If a customer is just buying tyres or wheels over the counter without bringing a vehicle:
1. On the Job Card screen, switch the top toggle to **Courier / Direct Sale**.
2. Enter the **Customer Name**, **Phone Number**, and **Shipping/Billing Address**.
3. Tap **Add Product** to pick the Tyres or Alloy Wheels from the catalog.
4. Tap **Submit** and proceed to billing.

---

## 💡 Quick Tips
* **No Camera on Computer?** If you ever test on a PC web browser, wheel photos are optional and can be skipped.
* **Need Help?** Contact your workshop supervisor or admin if your branch or user credentials need an update.
